Shorter bars are better. compile execute Each kernel runs through the BC interpreter twice — once on the raw HIR and once after the optimization pipeline. The pipeline includes const-folding, CSE, load-CSE, leaf inlining, LICM, loop and reduction vectorization, CFG simplification, alloca → malloc promotion, and drop-site insertion. Output is pinned per run; a future optimisation that silently changes the result fails this workflow loudly.
